3.1.1 In-detail - the Aristotle’s Hylomorphism

Per the subject topic, of which more philosophical than not, in Ainsworth (2016) it was to be said that Aristotle’s hylomorphism is rather advanced for its time. The philosophical doctrine of such posits that all physical entities are compounds of matter (hyle - the material constituent or potentiality) and form (morphe or eidos - the structure essence or actuality), to explain of the material and the world.

The notion is toward what are essentially compositional thought, for Aristotle of his general ideas. Aristotle defines matter as to be ‘out of which’ something is made. For example, letters are the matter of syllables. Thus, matter is a relative term: an object counts as matter relative to something else. Clay is matter relative to a brick because a brick is made of clay, at that specific point, whereas bricks are matter relative to a brick house. Change is analyzed as a material transformation, for where matter is what undergoes a change of form. In all, it is also the relational composition that marks the strength of this approach, and perhaps even now this line of thought has only been refined, much not disappeared entirely, because of the act it allows that would be central to pretty much the way down toward atomic scale, and so on.

#### Prout's hypothesis and the atomic weight problem (1815–1860s)

The *prime matter* now returned with the flawed model of such, as we found out, but now supported by *empirical patterns* that would seem to say validate the experiment and hypothesis. But so are many prime matter theory coming before it on itself. Nevertheless, it can be regarded as the prime matter crack of the lower-level concern for the Daltonian framework. Prout is asking Act IV, internally within Dalton's own frameworks. Specifically speaking, Dalton presupposed the atomic species. However, Prout hypothesized that it is not indeed the atomic species but simply different variations of the hydrogen atoms that made up the world on and by itself, thus the model of atomic structure is instead hydrogen-base, atomically composite of hydrogen atoms instead. 

The action itself is not wrong. Indeed, when we consider the hydrogen atom, it can be said to be the simplest ever atom observed of the physical reality with its configurations. Implicitly, the Daltonian framework does not reject Prout's hypothesis, but simply just giving a *justification of compositions* for where all the atomic differentiation between different atomic species are from, in the form being the answer as *modularity*, where the simplest structure helps itself to create a newer theory. 

Let us deploy the hypothesis. Suppose that we have the set of $\mathcal{N}\equiv\{N\}$ chemical elements. Then, Daltonian framework says that they differ three fundamental categories. First, mass $m$, second, size $v$ if we are to speak of volumetric adherence, and third, $p_{1},...,p_{n}$ of certain other properties. Because of the aforementioned compound problem, we suppose the properties list would be *complexity-dependent* that would emerge inconsistently and fundamentally plural given its circumstances and setup of the empirical usages. Then, we focus on $m$ and $v$. Because $v$ as we now know, is approximately small on the order of $\mu m$, my Daltonian scale, they can be said to approximately negligible as the choice of comparison. Then, the question remains would be to distinguish one singular element from another. This pushes the question to the **atom** itself as to ask *what is the atom*? More specifically, can there be existence of the *simplest possible atom* that would explain for how it was formed? That is Prout's direction. The other direction would be the *anti-modularity formalists* in the sense of non-collapse into atom composition, but to say that in general, the atoms differ like balloon - some can be big, some can be small, but they are *configured that way* and of the same *concentration* mechanics. This foregrounds the extrapolation outside the framework, implicitly, but internally of Daltonian framework, such allows for saying of the special element $N$ from $M$, thus validates Daltonian than being extracted of the implication. Daltonian modularity search comes with a problem. How do you presuppose the singular atom? There are many choices for that. For example, $\mathrm{He}$ for helium is with currently specified atomic mass to be around $4.002602$. By the time of Daltonian precision, that equates to simply $4$. Oxygen is $15.9994$, approximately still to be $16$. Then, indeed, we can say that helium constitutes oxygen. Which is weird, because Helium cannot form Nitrogen or Boron because of the whole number requirement. Lithium (for $\mathrm{Li}$ to be $\sim 6.941$) would be close to forming Neon, as $\mathrm{Ne}\sim 20.1797$. The pattern is inconsistent, but it is rather clear from experiments, they are non-trivially large. The only candidate that would be feasible under such thinking of such, without asking the blocked move of what is even the atom, would be to take *hydrogen*, which is of approximately $1.00794$ atomic mass. The number is almost too nice, and for the precision of the time, such can be said to simple one. That is where Prout was, and how the conjecture was formed. Surprisingly however, the issue is self-referential, using its own assumptions to immunize itself against the question those assumptions generated, of which would be seen in its response against Prout. 

The Daltonian framework, by then, responded by rejecting Prout empirically. A series of more careful measurements of the atomic weights, compiled by Berzelius in 1828 and Turner in 1832, disproved quite so of the hypothesis - in particular of the atomic weight of chlorine, the proportion is $35.45$ instead, which is not whole number as said. In fact, Berzelius, the foremost analytical chemist that compiled such, said in his work to be:

> This work belongs to those few productions from which science will derive no advantage whatever [...] The greatest civility which his contemporaries can show the author, is to forget that it was ever published.

Ironically, however, the result of being $35.45$ times that of Hydrogen worked against his favour, of which would be our current atomic model of current time. Suppose that the process we have contains a chemical reaction, specifically, between chlorine, oxygen, and hydrogen. Then, it is true that oxygen and hydrogen satisfies the trivial $k/p$-ratio requirement of chemical operation. However, this means that chlorine would not. Hence, the ordering matter, which means that same compositional chemistry, different history, different impossibility result. Furthermore, if oxygen and hydrogen reaction under certain specificity produces $\mathrm{H_{2}O}$, in which dilute and *react* with chlorine to form hypochlorous acid and hydrochloric acid, then it means that the dissemination of resolution increases - the reactant must include a sub-process, and why such exists is not known. Moreover, as we know of current form, chlorine reacts vigorously with hydrogen to produce hydrogen chloride gas. This means this direct argument glossed over the inconsistency in favour of conservatism that would expand the crack in another place rather than close it - we missed an entire process, and how the process works would not be known exactly. In a sense, Berzelius invited himself to a Turkey shoot of Dalton's leg. 

In the end, Prout's question was not answered. It was suppressed by more precise atomic weight measurements that appeared to refute it, but unknowingly, also widen the cracks. Furthermore, the discovery of *isotope* in much later covered this in much specificity - if the condition of neutron composition is good enough and calculated enough, no matter stability, then **any** element is able to be the baseline hydrogen is.
